Tier Breach Analyzer

Sankey view of privileged assignment paths from EntraOps Privileged EAM data. A tier breach is a path where an object classified on a lower (less privileged) tier reaches a service on a higher tier through a role assignment — the headline case being a Tier 1 / Tier 2 object reaching a Tier 0 (Control Plane) service. Click a node to trace its connections and filter the breach table.
