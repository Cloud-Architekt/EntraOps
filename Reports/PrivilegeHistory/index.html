Privilege History

Historic trends of privileged assets, role assignments and tier breaches over time, built from the git history of your EntraOps Privileged EAM export. Filter by RBAC System, RBAC Tier Level and Role, pick a time range, click a point on any chart to inspect that snapshot, and compare any two points in time (or a point vs. today's live data via the EAM Dashboard).

No Privilege History dataset found. Generate data/privilege-history-data.js from your repository's git history first:

Import-Module ./EntraOps -Force; New-EntraOpsPrivilegedEamPrivilegeHistoryData

Then reload this page. See EntraOpsConfig.json › PrivilegeHistory.EnablePrivilegeHistory to control whether New-EntraOpsReportingData generates this dataset automatically.
