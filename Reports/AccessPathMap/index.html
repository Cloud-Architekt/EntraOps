Access Path Map

APM-style graph exploration of the EntraOps OpenGraph model: the same nodes and edges the BloodHound integration ingests — principals, roles, role assignments, scopes, administrative units, devices and their classification enrichment. A tier breach attack path follows the Tier Breach Analyzer rules: a principal on a lower (less privileged) tier reaching a higher-tier service through a role assignment. A known attack path is a different, narrower signal: the role or role assignment matches a documented technique from the Classification Explorer's curated Attack Paths catalog — a path can be a tier breach without being documented, and vice versa. Click nodes and edges for full details.
